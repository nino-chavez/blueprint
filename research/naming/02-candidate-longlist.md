## Clearance-aware second round

The first-round leaders were eliminated by exact or adjacent collisions. A
second pass avoided saturated roots and focused on the differentiated concept of
methodological learning.

| Candidate | Strength | Primary weakness | Preliminary result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Remitry** | Bounded delegation plus learning from execution. | Pronunciation cue and remittance association. | No exact software collision found in this pass; test, do not select. |
| **Methodkeep** | Durable, self-improving shared methodology. | Sounds like a repository rather than an end-to-end method. | No exact software collision found in this pass; strongest new candidate. |
| **Worklore** | Accumulating operational memory. | “Lore” can feel informal. | Killed: active AI career and workplace products. |
| **Converna** | Convergence plus governance. | Coined spelling and possible “Coverna” mishearing. | Killed: existing Converna AI engine. |
| **Reasonkeep** | Custody of rationale across handoffs. | Narrows the product toward decision memory. | No exact software collision found in this pass; test, do not select. |
| **Rigora** | Disciplined, verified execution. | Pharmaceutical sound and weak learning signal. | Killed: multiple active software, AI, QA, and consulting uses. |
| **Accrete** | Capability strengthens through accumulation. | Awkward product noun; geology/dental associations. | Common/descriptive; not advanced. |
| **Workcanon** | Field-tested decisions become shared method. | Sounds rigid and doctrinal. | Killed: active work-management SaaS. |

## Language test

The minimum spoken and operating constructions are:

- “We run our initiatives through ___.”
- “___ is our product-development methodology.”
- “Run ___ Doctor before sharing.”
- “The team upgraded ___ this week.”
- `___ init`, `___ review`, `___ doctor`, and `___.yml`

Blueprint remains materially more natural across all six uses than the cleanest
new candidates.
