# Finalist scorecard and recommendation

This scorecard is additional evidence for the accepted retain-Blueprint decision
in `decisions/04-naming-reconfirmation.md`. It uses a separate candidate corpus
and does not create a competing canonical decision.

## Summary

No new candidate clears both the kill criteria and the rename threshold.

The recommended near-term architecture is:

- **Brand:** Blueprint
- **Issuer:** by Nino Chavez Labs
- **Descriptor:** Evidence-gated product initiatives, agent-assisted
- **Preferred domain pattern:** `useblueprint.dev`, subject to registrar confirmation
- **CLI/config:** retain `blueprint` and `blueprint.yml`

This is a retention decision with explicit conditions, not a claim that
Blueprint is commercially ownable. If independent commercial brand ownership
becomes a requirement, naming should reopen rather than pretending a modifier
domain solves the word-mark problem.

margin. No candidate reaches it, and none has yet passed real cold-reader or
listener testing.

## Why the comparatively clean candidates still lose

### Methodkeep

Best new candidate in this sprint. It is clear, pronounceable, and expresses
durable methodology. It still sounds like a repository of methods rather than an
end-to-end operating method covering research, prototype, validation, portal,
fleet, and governance. `methodkeep doctor` is workable but less natural than
`blueprint doctor`.

### Remitry

Best expression of bounded delegation. It is distinctive, but requires a
pronunciation cue (`REH-mit-ree`), invites spelling errors, and carries financial
“remittance” associations. A name that needs teaching does not justify migrating
an already fluent CLI.

### Reasonkeep

Strong on rationale custody and handoff continuity. It narrows the product toward
decision memory and under-represents active research, prototyping, stage
progression, deployment, and fleet operation.

## Adversarial language result

Qualified Blueprint remains superior in natural use:

- “We run our product initiatives through Blueprint.”
- “Blueprint gives agents the pipeline and verification steps.”
- “Run Blueprint Doctor before sharing the portal.”
- `blueprint init`, `blueprint stage status`, `blueprint review`,
  `blueprint doctor`, `blueprint.yml`.

The descriptor supplies what the noun hides: evidence gates, agent assistance,
and whole-initiative scope. The issuer supplies provenance. Neither cures legal
genericness, but both improve comprehension and search association without
discarding operational equity.

## Reopen triggers

Reopen naming when any of these becomes true:

1. An independent commercial entity or paid platform is being formed.
2. Enterprise procurement reports actual confusion with Pega Blueprint or
   Blueprint Software Systems.
3. Search, package, or command ambiguity measurably blocks adoption.
4. Formal trademark counsel advises that the planned use is materially exposed.
5. A candidate passes the 84-point threshold, preliminary clearance, and real
   pronunciation/spelling testing.

## Required real-audience test before any future rename

Test the incumbent plus at most four finalists with 8–12 target users. Require:

- At least 80% correct pronunciation from text.
- At least 80% correct spelling after hearing the name.
- Unaided recall after 20 minutes.
- Correct category inference without explanation.
- Comfort saying “We run our initiatives through ___.”
- No material reduction in enterprise credibility versus Blueprint.

Preference is secondary to comprehension, recall, and trust.
