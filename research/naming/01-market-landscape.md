# Naming market landscape

## Finding

**Blueprint remains semantically strong but is commercially difficult to own.**
The collision is not merely that “blueprint” is a common noun. Several active
products use it in the same AI-assisted product discovery, requirements, and
software-delivery neighborhood.

Retention is defensible while this remains a qualified open-source methodology
associated with Nino Chavez. It becomes strategically weak if the ambition is an
independent commercial master brand with unambiguous word-of-mouth, search, or
CLI ownership.

## Direct and adjacent collisions

- [Pega Blueprint](https://www.pega.com/blueprint) is an AI-assisted enterprise
  application-design product spanning discovery through production, with
  governance and agents doing substantial work. Pega also publishes a
  [Blueprint Delivered methodology](https://community.pega.com/project/blueprint-delivered).
- [`@lipter7/blueprint`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/%40lipter7/blueprint) is
  an active npm package for context engineering and spec-driven development. It
  includes research, planning, subagent orchestration, human oversight, and a
  `blueprint` command.
- [AI-First Blueprint](https://www.falcux.com/en/ai-first-blueprint) is a
  tool-agnostic methodology for teams building with AI agents and persistent
  artifacts.
- [Blueprint Software Systems](https://www.blueprintsys.com/) remains an active
  enterprise automation/process company with historical requirements-authoring,
  review, validation, and signoff products.
- [Palantir Blueprint](https://github.com/palantir/blueprint) dominates
  developer-package search through the `@blueprintjs/*` ecosystem.
- [Blueprint Framework](https://github.com/blueprintframework/framework) is a
  separate open-source framework with a `blueprint` CLI.
- Large adjacent uses include [Unreal Engine
  Blueprints](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/blueprints-visual-scripting-in-unreal-engine),
  [Mozilla.ai Blueprints](https://blueprints.mozilla.ai/), and the historical
  [Azure Blueprints](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/governance/blueprints/overview).

The closest collision is Pega: its current language—AI-speed application design,
discovery-to-production, enterprise governance, and agents doing the work—is
close to the story Blueprint would tell in an enterprise evaluation.

## Preliminary trademark signals

This is not legal advice or a freedom-to-operate opinion.

- Pegasystems filed **PEGA BLUEPRINT**, US serial 99315910, in 2025 for
  AI/software-development, workflow, and digital-transformation goods and
  services ([indexed record](https://trademarks.justia.com/993/15/pega-99315910.html)).
- Blueprint Software Systems has a Canadian word registration, TMA751221, for
  software used in analysis, development, and testing
  ([official CIPO record](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/1357002?wbdisable=true)).
- Its former US exact-word registration was cancelled in 2020. Cancellation
  does not establish freedom to use because live market use, pending
  applications, common-law rights, and other jurisdictions remain relevant.

## SpecifAI finding

`specifai.dev` should not become this product's identity.

- [Specifai.io](https://specifai.io/) is an AI-powered SDLC platform for BRDs,
  PRDs, NFRs, user stories, test cases, process visualization, integrations, and
  strategic initiatives.
- Its active public [GitHub repository](https://github.com/presidio-oss/specif-ai)
  uses the same positioning.
- [Specifai.ai](https://specifai.ai/) is a separate active product.

This is a direct category and phonetic collision.

## Saturated naming territories

- Construction: Blueprint, Forge, Foundry, Scaffold, Framework.
- Navigation/control: Compass, Helm, Waypoint, Rail, Railway, Path.
- Verification as bare nouns: Proof, Trace, Audit, Provenance.
- Obvious AI puns: SpecifAI, VerifAI, ClarifAI-style formations.
- Generic platform nouns: Flow, Loop, Stack, Hub, Layer, Fabric, Portal, Atlas.

The stronger remaining territory is the product's most unusual idea:
methodological learning. Each failure becomes durable infrastructure inherited
by the next initiative. Related territory includes custody of rationale, bounded
delegation, disciplined convergence, and accumulating operational memory.

## Retention conditions

Retention is defensible when:

- The product is consistently qualified by issuer and descriptor.
- Exclusive word-mark and command ownership are not immediate requirements.
- Coexistence with other Blueprint tools is accepted.

Retention becomes weak when:

- Commercial category ownership becomes a primary goal.
- The product needs unambiguous enterprise procurement, search, or word-of-mouth.
- A modifier domain is expected to cure word-mark confusion. It improves
  navigation, not distinctiveness.

## Repository switching cost

The name currently appears in 329 repository files, including 161 distribution
files under `template/`. It is embedded in the published
`@nino-chavez-labs/blueprint-cli` package, the `blueprint` binary,
`blueprint.yml`, environment/config conventions, portal URLs, reviewer names,
wave history, and the 13-consumer registry.

That cost is manageable through aliases, but it raises the standard: a new name
must create material strategic advantage, not merely freshness.
