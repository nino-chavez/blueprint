## The experiment establishes a narrow mechanical result

`checks.json` records the actual controls. The current design-document gate's missing-file control fails, then a lone design document passes. The prototype separately rejects missing journeys/pages/interactions/permissions/states, incomplete state dispositions, unresolved permission decisions, invalid transitions, stale inputs, unverified human authority and missing freeze/build permission.

An integration control uses a disposable copy of the current stage engine with its private registry exported. It confirms that a stale definition blocks the derived prefix and `recordAdvance`, even with a recorded completed cursor and an assertion aimed at the mechanical gate. No source engine file is edited.

These results are evidence that the experimental checks see those defects in an invented fixture. They are not proof of a complete real application definition, of an adopted phase schema, of a functioning production authority adapter, or of better product outcomes. The RFC's prospective pilot and acceptance gates remain open.
