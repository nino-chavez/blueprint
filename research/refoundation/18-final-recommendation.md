# Blueprint re-foundation final recommendation

## Outcome

Refound Blueprint's semantics; do not rewrite the whole product.

The completed evidence supports a side-by-side clean semantic core with compact
authoring and compatibility adapters. The root self-application adopts that
contract through Decision 08; Fleet Observability and Film Room now have
committed native pilot branches with contrasting operating boundaries. Existing
stages, reviewers, portals, doctor checks, and distribution machinery remain
capabilities above or beside the core until evidence justifies migration or
retirement.

Do not publish the research schema or change stamped defaults yet. Film Room's
native migration is structurally complete, but its founder operation receipt
and the platform support/compatibility gates for public distribution remain
open.

The longitudinal Film Room audit narrows this conclusion further. The
refounded core is validated as an evidence-control kernel; a complete
end-to-end steering method is not yet demonstrated. The native contract kept
claims honest and revisions isolated, but the decisive UX strategy was
produced by a separate Steering plan while Blueprint was frozen. The live
contract also grew from 164 to 644 lines across the candidate history. Public
distribution therefore requires evidence that recipes above the kernel improve
prospective work selection and remain comprehensible over time, not merely
that the evaluator compiles correctly.

A pre-registered root-only experiment now provides the first bounded evidence
for that recipe layer. Five fixture-scale packets selected the expected
implementation, holistic-audit, repair, refresh, and disposition recipes, and
the adversarial harness passed 74 assertions. This supports continued dogfood;
it does not cross the distribution threshold because only Blueprint's own
implementation was prospective and the three consumer comparisons were frozen
retrospective packets.

## Direct disposition of the original questions

### Claude, the method, or both?

Both, but the method owns prevention.

Claude made local choices in Film Room that over-weighted portal/provenance work
and let intermediate proof carry too much meaning. The then-current method made
those choices look procedurally valid because progress, output lifecycle, and
proof grades were not bound tightly enough to exact founder/product claims.

Codex identified and re-steered the mismatch. That does not establish that
Codex is intrinsically safe or Claude intrinsically unsuitable. A capable agent
under an ambiguous contract can repeat the failure. Model-specific execution
adapters can improve performance; correctness cannot depend on the model name.

### Is Blueprint correctly defined as a method for steering agentic work?

That phrase is useful shorthand but not the formal category. It over-centers
the executor and sounds prompt-adjacent.

Use:

> Blueprint is a repository-native, evidence-steered initiative protocol for
> human-authorized, agent-executed work.

Blueprint governs an initiative across agents, operators, stakeholders, tools,
external systems, and time. It steers through chartered authority, falsifiable
claims, compatible receipts, deterministic state, and explicit dispositions.

### Is Blueprint the right name?

Keep **Blueprint** as the brand, with the qualified descriptor above.

The literal metaphor is imperfect: the contract is a revisable control loop,
not a fixed up-front construction plan. No tested replacement has enough
clarity and migration advantage to justify a rename. Reopen naming only when
cold target users repeatedly infer fixed specification, waterfall planning, or
document generation after seeing the descriptor, or when Decision 04's
commercial threshold is reached.

## First-principles rebuild: demonstrated advantage

Rebuilding the semantic layer from first principles delivered measurable
benefit:

1. **Historical ceremony became optional.** Stages, portals, tiers, and handoff
   were not copied into the kernel merely because they existed.
2. **Evidence became claim-specific.** Open, stale, contradicted,
   unobservable, invalidated, and satisfied are different states with reasons.
3. **Authority became executable.** Agents cannot silently expand intent, and
   mistaken receipts require exact correction authority.
4. **Model identity left the correctness boundary.** Claude, Codex, humans,
   scheduled systems, and tool adapters participate through the same evidence
   contract.
5. **Authoring became smaller without semantic loss.** Three consumers fell
   from 642 to 143 nonblank lines (77.7%); the root fell from 116 to 40 (65.5%).
6. **A cold author could use it.** The isolated exercise passed 34/34 in two
   attempts, zero questions, and zero creator intervention.
7. **False green became visible in a real consumer.** Fleet simultaneously
   reports implementation satisfied, report arrival satisfied, report use
   contradicted, and legacy doctor contradicted instead of flattening them into
   one progress label.

## First-principles rebuild: demonstrated disadvantage

The complexity did not disappear; it moved into accountable infrastructure:

- compiler and parser behavior;
- versioned actor/evidence profiles;
- schema migrations and source-located diagnostics;
- append-only receipt and correction UX;
- compatibility adapters and dual-view source ownership;
- public support and rollback windows; and
- the risk that convenient profiles become hidden methodology.

A complete greenfield product rewrite would add these obligations while
discarding years of current-tool edge cases. The strangler approach therefore
has a better risk/learning ratio: rebuild the semantic center, adapt proven
capability, and migrate one consumer boundary at a time.

## Rollout recommendation

1. Keep root `blueprint-native.yml` authoritative under Decision 08.
2. Keep Fleet's pilot branch and its append-only arrival/non-use receipts. A
   later actual-use encounter must remain distinct and may require an explicit
   disposition if it targets the same current claim.
3. Do not change `template/` or public CLI defaults yet.
4. Treat the completed Film Room blind A/B as method-comparison evidence, not a
   live migration: both arms steered correctly, while native led by three
   points—below the preregistered four-point threshold.
5. Keep Film Room's native pilot branch at `0db4285` and all three failed
   candidate histories immutable. Keep `5496edd` / `a370647` as the preserved
   operator-onboarding boundary and `acbc2d0` / `9c22428` as the active,
   operator-deferred observation boundary. Do not reinterpret package, browser,
   coached, simulated, earlier human, or later-product proof across revisions.
6. Migrate BC Subscriptions last, after state/scenario freshness generation and
   receiving-team authority are automated.
7. Consider an opt-in public experimental command only after two contrasting
   live pilots, upgrade/doctor/fleet support, a declared compatibility window,
   and demonstrated encounter-readiness, incident-clustering, active-history
   projection, and longitudinal-burden controls.
8. Treat exact UX-closure product `acbc2d0` / semantic tip `9c22428` as an open,
   operator-deferred observation boundary. Do not infer a founder result from
   native launch, accessibility-tree visibility, earlier encounters, or the
   newer `463bf0b` product candidate.
9. Permit only a read-only BC Subscriptions readiness audit while the Phase 4
   founder gate is deferred. A live authoritative Phase 5 migration requires a
   separate sequence change or waiver.
10. Keep the steering evaluator root-only. A public opt-in proposal requires a
    separately authorized prospective external pilot with a frozen packet,
    operator-touch budget, recipe transitions, and observed support/rollback
    behavior.

## Final judgment

The Film Room incident does not show that Blueprint should be abandoned or that
Claude should simply be replaced. It shows that Blueprint's then-current center
of gravity was wrong: too much process and output state, too little exact claim,
authority, and evidence semantics.

The re-foundation corrects that center without discarding the useful product.
Blueprint is best treated as an initiative protocol whose invariant is an
inspectable steering loop—not a portal, stage sequence, document set, or agent
brand. The root steering experiment shows that missing recipe controls can sit
above that invariant without changing it. Whether those controls improve a live
external initiative remains the next falsifiable question.
