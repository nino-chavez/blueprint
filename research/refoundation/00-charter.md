# Blueprint re-foundation charter

## Question

If Blueprint were derived today from first principles, using its consumer history
as evidence rather than treating its current architecture as a constraint, what
is the smallest operating method that would keep product-initiative claims,
authority, work, and evidence aligned while agents carry substantial execution?

This is a re-foundation study, not an assumed rewrite. Its first output is an
explanation of the field record. A new schema, stage model, CLI, template, or
name is not justified until a candidate model explains the record better than
the current method and survives falsification against materially different
initiative shapes.

## Why this study exists now

Film Room exposed two distinct failures that are easy to collapse into one:

1. **Conformance failure:** existing Blueprint rules were not followed. Doctor
   was not run until the operator asked; implementation began before the
   operator-reviewed prototype; the requirements spine was assembled
   retroactively.
2. **Coverage failure:** a founder-operated tool crossed into second-operator
   distribution, but the scope trigger did not mechanically require a new
   launch and lifecycle contract before that work advanced.

BC Subscriptions supplies the adjacent evidence: a deterministic register can
be internally correct while its status language outruns its oracle. Static
presence checks became "built and working" claims until the G1–G5 verification
ladder separated specification, prototype, presence, behavior, and live proof.

Together these cases make a clean runtime-vendor explanation insufficient.
They also make another amendment-only response risky: the current method may be
accumulating correct local controls without a small shared model that explains
when each control activates and what claim it can support.

## Working domain boundary

The evidence presently supports this domain:

> Product initiatives in which humans establish intent and authority, agents
> perform substantial research/design/build work, and advancement depends on
> evidence that named actor outcomes or operational claims have been satisfied.

The study does **not** assume Blueprint governs arbitrary coding tasks, agent
orchestration, model routing, or every form of knowledge work. Those may be
adjacent tools or conditional modules. Expanding beyond product initiatives
requires evidence from a consumer whose defining work cannot be described as a
product initiative.

## Unit of analysis

The unit is a **claim-control incident**, not a document, stage, portal, model,
or bad conversation turn.

An incident exists when an initiative:

- made or attempted a consequential progress/readiness/outcome claim;
- possessed evidence that supported, contradicted, or failed to support it;
- advanced, stopped, revised, or re-chartered because of a human, agent, gate,
  encounter, test, or external event; and
- can be examined for the earliest portable control that would have improved
  the result.

Positive controls count. A gate that correctly kills a pilot or a lean
initiative that avoids unnecessary ceremony is evidence equal to a false green.

## First-principles constraints

These are constraints on the study, not the preselected kernel:

1. **Claims cannot exceed their evidence.** Artifact existence, behavior, live
   operation, and observed-human outcomes are different claims.
2. **Authority is explicit.** Producing work does not automatically authorize
   the producer to certify its judgment or a human outcome.
3. **Intent can change without rewriting history.** Prior evidence remains true
   for its prior scope; a new scope may require new claims and evidence.
4. **Ceremony activates on evidenced need.** A solo local tool does not inherit
   acquisition, distribution, support, or handoff work merely because another
   consumer needed it.
5. **Failure attribution is classified before promotion.** Existing-rule
   noncompliance, unenforced rules, missing method, insufficient or stale
   evidence, changed intent, tooling defects, and project-specific exceptions
   are not the same failure.
6. **The method is runtime-agnostic.** Claude, Codex, a human team, or another
   runtime may expose a weakness; no vendor-specific behavior becomes a kernel
   rule without a controlled comparison and a portable underlying invariant.
7. **Current Blueprint is evidence, not authority.** Existing stages, variants,
   portals, reviewers, and terminology must earn their place in the new model.

## Corpus

The primary corpus is deliberately stratified:

| Initiative | Distinct shape under test |
|---|---|
| Film Room | founder-operated feasibility and production → distributable second-operator product |
| BC Subscriptions | specification/presence → behavior/live verification in a large code-shipping initiative |
| Rally HQ | live midstream SaaS, active stakeholders, state drift, and reader-facing projections |
| SE Docs Front Door | configure-first, externally hosted product surface, multiple human actors, no-code primary path |
| QuantifAI | pilot invalidation and explicit re-chartering before build commitment |
| Fleet Observability | lean solo dogfood control that should not pay external-product ceremony |
| external adopter | transfer beyond the creator's workspace and tacit knowledge, if source evidence is accessible |

All other registered or locally discovered consumers enter the capability map.
They receive a full incident replay only when they introduce a new initiative
shape or falsify a conclusion from the primary corpus.

## Success criteria for a candidate kernel

A candidate earns an adoption comparison only if it:

- catches every ratified critical incident at or before the earliest safe point;
- preserves successful prior claims when scope changes rather than treating the
  whole initiative as failed;
- makes the evidence ceiling of every green state inspectable;
- prevents a builder from self-issuing human/judgment receipts;
- represents configured/external behavior and non-code work without pretending
  a repository file is the product;
- keeps the lean control materially lean;
- uses no consumer-specific or runtime-specific primitive;
- maps existing evidence forward without requiring consumers to recreate it;
- can be explained to a cold practitioner in five minutes; and
- has executable negative fixtures for its highest-risk transitions.

## Disqualifiers

Stop or revise the re-foundation if any candidate:

- requires universal lifecycle or stakeholder artifacts for the lean control;
- catches known incidents only through consumer-specific exceptions;
- makes the external surface or portal the universal output again;
- treats simulated/cold-agent proof as observed-human success;
- resolves ambiguity by adding a new document rather than a claim, authority,
  evidence, or transition rule;
- loses the field-earned protections it claims to simplify; or
- becomes a general agent operating system without consumer evidence for that
  expansion.

## Decision gates

1. **Corpus ratification:** the operator agrees that the incident records
   describe what happened, including alternative explanations and positive
   controls.
2. **Kernel falsification:** a candidate survives counterfactual replays and
   negative fixtures without burdening the lean control.
3. **Architecture choice:** compare amend-v1, compatibility-layer v2, and clean
   rewrite. Elegance is not a decision criterion by itself.
4. **Pilot authorization:** only a ratified candidate may be prototyped in the
   root self-application with dual validation.
5. **Distribution authorization:** `template/` and external consumers remain
   untouched until pilots pass and the fleet/freeze check is clean.

## Evidence and publication rules

- This repository is public. Employer, counterparty, customer, credential, and
  private-repository identities remain de-named.
- Session JSONLs are evidence locations, not content to reproduce wholesale.
  Store only the minimum public-safe excerpt or finding needed for the incident.
- Agent-generated audits are candidate evidence until sampled against canonical
  source, code, schema, git history, or observed behavior.
- A fresh evaluator's disagreement with a builder is an independent-review
  signal, not proof of a vendor-level model difference.
- Research files in this directory are non-canonical until an explicit decision
  ratifies a kernel or disposition.

## Non-goals for the evidence sprint

- No `METHODOLOGY.md` edits.
- No `template/` edits.
- No CLI, stage-machine, reviewer, hook, or schema implementation.
- No consumer restamps or migrations.
- No rename decision.
- No claim that the current method is failed or that a rewrite is preferred.

## Expected evidence-sprint outputs

1. This charter.
2. A reusable incident-record contract.
3. A corpus index that distinguishes registry metadata from verified local
   evidence.
4. One dossier per primary case, including positive controls and alternate
   causal explanations.
5. An operator ratification list: disputed facts, missing evidence, and the
   smallest set of incidents the future kernel must catch.

Only after those outputs close does first-principles kernel derivation begin.
