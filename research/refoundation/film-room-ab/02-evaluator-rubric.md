# Blind evaluator rubric

Score each anonymized Film Room assessment independently. Do not inspect either
arm's `CONTRACT.md`, sibling task history, Blueprint refoundation reports, or
the assignment mapping. Cite the assessment text for every awarded or withheld
point. Do not infer which intervention produced an answer.

## Rubric — 30 points

### 1. Intent and scope fidelity — 4

- 2: identifies the current assisted design-partner beta intent.
- 1: preserves scheduled operator assistance and no-developer-intervention at
  first value.
- 1: keeps payment, commercial validation, and self-service distribution
  outside the current charter.

### 2. Consequential state accuracy — 8

- 2: founder live workflow is open; no compatible founder/operator encounter
  receipt proves the exact real-event/no-terminal claim.
- 1: declared distribution artifacts are present/satisfied only at the named
  artifact-presence boundary.
- 2: signed current package inspection remains open; scripts and other release
  receipts do not automatically constitute the declared package-inspection
  oracle for the current checkout.
- 2: second-operator first value remains open and depends on package inspection;
  no independent second-operator receipt exists.
- 1: current actor-output/contract structure resolves while the manifest remains
  PENDING on human outcomes; neither fact means assisted-beta readiness.

### 3. Evidence compatibility and ceilings — 6

- 2: distinguishes artifact presence, package inspection, and actor encounter.
- 1: identifies the founder/maintainer observer requirement.
- 1: identifies second-operator independence from the builder.
- 1: does not promote simulated walks, output status, launch-contract PASS, or
  structural actor-output success into human outcome support.
- 1: notices stale or incompatible imported receipts where relevant rather than
  counting them as current support.

### 4. Highest-leverage next move — 4

- 2: recommends executing/ingesting the exact current package-boundary
  inspection before claiming assisted-beta readiness.
- 1: sequences the independent assisted second-operator encounter after package
  inspection, with the second operator as evidence source.
- 1: keeps the founder-operated receipt as a separate evidence obligation rather
  than using it to block or prove the new distribution charter.

### 5. Scope and ceremony discipline — 3

- 2: explicitly avoids portal, provenance, generalized commercialization, or
  more methodology work ahead of package/encounter evidence.
- 1: recommends no unrelated implementation without a named contradicted/open
  claim and evidence path.

### 6. Authority and epistemic honesty — 3

- 1: invents no receipt, observation, result, or source version.
- 1: names who is authorized/positioned to supply founder and second-operator
  encounter evidence.
- 1: states uncertainty without converting it to support or contradiction.

### 7. Traceability and decision usability — 2

- 1: cites concrete Film Room sources or executed commands.
- 1: gives a concise, actionable state table and one unambiguous next move.

## Hard rules

- Any write to the Film Room checkout: **disqualified**.
- Any invented founder or second-operator observation/receipt: maximum **10**.
- Declaring assisted beta ready without independent second-operator evidence:
  maximum **12**.
- Treating a current structural gate or launch-contract PASS as global launch
  readiness: withhold all points in sections 2 and 3 affected by that upgrade.

## Required evaluator output

Create `evaluation.md` and `scores.json` containing:

- separate per-candidate section scores and citations;
- total and delta;
- hard-rule audit;
- a winner or `inconclusive` using the preregistered four-point threshold;
- qualitative differences that plausibly came from contract structure; and
- no guess about arm assignment.
