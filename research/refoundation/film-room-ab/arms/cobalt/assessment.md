## Governing intent and scope ceiling

The governing intent is to turn the existing founder-operated Film Room into a **local-first, assisted design-partner beta for a second solo sports-media operator**. The concrete launch promise is a direct, notarized macOS-arm64 DMG that a second operator can install on a clean machine, configure without a terminal, use to learn the Review → Pause → Decide → Handoff loop on synthetic footage, and then use for useful local output and a real event without engineer intervention. `blueprint.yml` fixes the first-value path as `install-sample-review-one-clip-generate-local-output`; `launch-contract.yml` fixes the 20-minute activation budget and zero-intervention target.

This is a midstream portability/commercialization effort against a working product, not a greenfield expansion. ADR-0007 scopes diagnosis to the standalone-app blast radius. The first beta includes generic local ingest, review, render, report, handoff, setup, recovery, export, and exit. The ceiling excludes auto-publishing, a hosted substrate, the founder gallery/photography repository, founder launchd/browser-wrapper integration, and the Mac App Store while the GPL-dependent media stack remains. Public self-service acquisition, payments, accounts, scalable support, and an updater are later launch levels. The pilot also excludes coach tactical scouting, parent live-stream sales, and recruiting-profile workflows. The club director's broader automation interest and an issued scope aid do not silently enlarge this product boundary.

## Highest-leverage next move

Run the **first evidence-bearing release-candidate encounter**, in dependency order, without changing scope: the maintainer who controls the Apple Developer identity should turn the already verified unsigned payload into one exact signed, notarized, stapled DMG; record its hashes, nested/outer signature checks, notarization and Gatekeeper results; and inspect the bundled runtime, approved sample, release manifest, and readable FFmpeg corresponding-source evidence inside that exact candidate. After the outstanding native P0/P1 acceptance checks pass on that same candidate, a named second solo sports-media operator—not the builder—should execute the existing cold-install receipt uncoached on a clean account or Mac.

The maintainer/release owner can supply the cryptographic, package-inspection, native-lifecycle, and support-contact evidence. Only the second operator, observed without explanatory intervention, can supply the comprehension, recovery, timing, first-output, and real-event evidence. This move is higher leverage than adding product surface because it converts the largest current ambiguity—verified construction versus transferable value—into falsifiable receipts.

## Work that should explicitly not happen next

- Do not add more AI, detector scope, workflow breadth, or club marketing automation before the existing second-operator path is observed.
- Do not build the public marketing/payment/account stack, an updater, invitation-beta machinery, or the Mac App Store variant for this gate.
- Do not distribute the audit app or ad-hoc-signed release app, bypass Gatekeeper, or use staged Chromium evidence as a native package receipt.
- Do not retire `/classic` until a full current-console founder event clears the ADR-0004 trigger.
- Do not relabel `issued`, `ready`, a validator PASS, director interest, or operator assistance as human outcome evidence. In particular, do not clear the three manifest pendings without the specified guest/director encounters.
- Do not widen the beta to auto-publishing, hosted storage, founder-only integrations, coach scouting, parent streaming, or recruiting profiles.
