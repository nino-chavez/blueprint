## Qualitative contract-structure differences

- Amber is organized around explicit claim/evidence ceilings. It names the four
  distribution programs at the artifact-presence boundary, calls out a stale
  derived view and an incompatible cross-workflow encounter, and states the
  scheduled-support and commercial-validation ceilings verbatim.
- Cobalt supplies a richer lifecycle and product-scope narrative, more package
  detail, and a broader source inventory. That structure still reaches the same
  five boundary states and next move, but it leaves scheduled assistance,
  commercial-validation exclusion, and the founder/maintainer observer rule
  implicit or unstated.
- Both structures successfully prevent structural or simulated evidence from
  becoming human-outcome support. The principal qualitative difference is
  explicitness of semantic ceilings versus breadth of lifecycle context, not a
  disagreement about whether the beta is ready.

These are candidate-level structural observations only; they do not identify or
guess treatment/control assignment.
