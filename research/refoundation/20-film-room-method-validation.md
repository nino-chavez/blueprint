# Film Room method-validation audit

## Verdict

Film Room validates the refounded Blueprint core as an **evidence-control
kernel**. It does not yet validate Blueprint as a complete end-to-end steering
method.

The native contract did four important things reliably:

1. kept automated, package, native-window, founder, and second-operator claims
   separate;
2. preserved contradictions instead of converting repaired code into retroactive
   success;
3. bound later evidence to exact product revisions; and
4. kept assisted-beta readiness contradicted while signing, support, launch,
   accessibility, and second-operator evidence remained incomplete.

Those are material improvements over the failure that triggered the
re-foundation. They establish a truthful control plane.

They do not show that the kernel selected the right product work early enough,
produced the later holistic UX strategy, or reduced the operator's steering
burden. Film Room's decisive UX audit and redesign ran under a separate
Steering plan while `blueprint-native.yml` was explicitly frozen. Blueprint
may consume that candidate as evidence; it cannot take credit for steering the
work that produced it.

The exact `acbc2d0` founder outcome is deliberately deferred. It remains
`open`: there is no pass, failure, substitute receipt, or inference from the
newer `463bf0b` product candidate.

## What was tested

This audit asks a different question from "does Film Room work?"

> Did Blueprint cause better initiative decisions prospectively, or did it
> mainly record the truth about decisions and outcomes produced elsewhere?

The unit is the control's contribution at the time a decision was made. A
later well-formed receipt cannot retroactively turn bookkeeping into steering.

## Hypothesis results

### H1 — Blueprint prevents evidence ceilings from being exceeded

**Pass.**

Browser tests never became native-window proof. Package inspection never became
founder proof. A native folder panel never became end-to-end operation. A
founder result never became second-operator activation. This held through
multiple repairs and a later UX candidate.

### H2 — Blueprint prevents candidate laundering

**Pass after a live defect and hardening.**

The initial pilot used current-source semantics that became ambiguous when
product repairs began. Commit `0db4285` introduced exact revision binding.
Every later candidate then received separate claims and receipts. The final
behavior is correct, but the control was learned reactively rather than present
in the first live contract.

### H3 — Blueprint makes authority executable

**Pass at the semantic boundary.**

Agents issued only declared machine/package receipts. The maintainer retained
human-outcome and correction authority. No agent-certified founder or
second-operator outcome entered the ledger.

### H4 — Blueprint selects the highest-leverage next work

**Partial.**

The blind A/B showed that both native and current contracts selected the same
package-before-operator order. During the live pilot, however, the single
`founder-live-workflow` claim was too coarse to expose first-run routing,
native-dialog capability, operator configuration, visible Settings return, and
end-to-end continuity before scarce founder time was used. Each contradiction
produced another local repair.

The separate Steering plan eventually clustered the incidents and required a
whole-journey UX audit. That was the higher-leverage intervention, but it was
not selected by the kernel or a validated Blueprint recipe.

### H5 — Blueprint lowers authoring and operator burden in sustained use

**Not demonstrated; longitudinal evidence currently contradicts a simple
low-burden claim.**

Research-scale compact replay reduced Film Room's initial explicit overlay to a
63-line compact source. The first committed live contract was 164 lines. As
candidate-specific claims and append-only receipts accumulated, it reached 523
lines at `a370647` and 644 lines at the resumed `acbc2d0` boundary. The number
of authored record entries measured by the same structural pattern grew from
16 to 60 to 75.

Between `83d85fc` and `a370647`, the pilot produced 13 commits: three product
repairs and ten migration, decision-status, candidate-binding, or evidence
commits. Agents authored most of this material, but the review and
comprehension surface still belongs to the operator. Initial compression is
not evidence of sustainable comprehensibility.

### H6 — Blueprint's correctness is model-independent

**Supported, not proven universally.**

The causal model no longer depends on Claude or Codex identities, and the cold
author plus multiple agent-produced receipts exercised the same semantics.
The paired Film Room assessment did not show a decisive performance difference.
This supports keeping model identity outside the correctness boundary. It does
not show that adapters, prompts, or model capability are irrelevant to
execution quality.

## What Blueprint is now justified to claim

Blueprint can claim:

> A repository-native protocol that keeps initiative intent, authority,
> consequential claims, evidence, and changes of direction inspectable while
> humans and agents execute the work.

It cannot yet claim:

> A validated end-to-end method that reliably chooses and sequences the best
> product work with lower operator burden.

That second promise needs tested steering recipes above the kernel. A protocol
can tell the truth about a poorly chosen plan; that does not make the plan good.

## Missing capabilities exposed by the pilot

These are method requirements before public experimental distribution, not
Film Room product requirements:

1. **Encounter readiness recipe.** Before consuming scarce human evidence,
   decompose the task's critical path and mechanically clear deterministic
   prerequisites. The human should test judgment, comprehension, or real
   operation—not discover a fixed port, missing local configuration, or an
   absent return control one at a time.
2. **Incident-cluster trigger.** Two contradictions along the same outcome
   path must force an explicit disposition between another local repair and a
   holistic journey/system audit.
3. **Active/historical projection.** Keep append-only source history, but
   generate a compact active view so an operator does not need to read every
   superseded candidate claim to understand current state.
4. **Operator-touch budget.** A plan must declare which observations truly
   require the operator and why. Repeated human reruns are a cost and a method
   signal, not free test execution.
5. **Steering-recipe evaluation.** Research, design, prototype, build, launch,
   and recovery recipes must be judged by prospective decision quality, not by
   artifact completion or compatibility with the kernel.
6. **Longitudinal burden metric.** Measure contract growth, review time,
   corrections, decisions, and operator interventions over a real initiative;
   initial line-count compression is insufficient.

## Consequences for the migration plan

1. Root adoption of the semantic core remains justified as an internal
   governing contract.
2. Film Room remains the second contrasting live **control-contract**
   migration, but its founder outcome is open and now explicitly deferred.
3. The newer `463bf0b` product candidate is a separate future boundary. It
   inherits no `acbc2d0` receipt and requires new authorization before
   Blueprint observation.
4. Phase 5 BC Subscriptions may receive a read-only readiness and
   counterfactual audit. A live authoritative migration is not earned while the
   Phase 4 human-outcome gate is deferred unless the operator explicitly
   changes or waives that sequence.
5. Phase 6 public experimental distribution is not earned. Two contract
   migrations and a cold-author pass are necessary but insufficient while the
   steering-recipe and sustained-burden results remain unresolved.
6. No `template/` or public CLI default change follows from this audit.

## Cross-consumer check

The Film Room result is not an isolated usability complaint:

- Fleet's first live encounter forced one coarse operation claim to split into
  arrival and use.
- BC Subscriptions' refreshed Phase 5 shadow remains valid while rejecting its
  stale state register, absent normalized scenario results, and unobserved
  receiving-team acceptance.
- The cold-author exercise proves small-contract initialization, not sustained
  maintenance after multiple candidate revisions.
- The Film Room blind A/B remains formally inconclusive.

The common validated behavior is truthful refusal and exact evidence scoping.
The common unresolved behavior is prospective claim decomposition, steering
recipe quality, and longitudinal operator burden. BC's detailed readiness
result is in `21-bc-subscriptions-phase5-readiness.md`.

## Name and category

Keep **Blueprint** as the brand. The audit strengthens the need for the precise
category:

> Evidence-steered initiative protocol for human-authorized, agent-executed
> work.

Calling it simply a "method for steering agentic work" currently overstates the
validated result. Blueprint has demonstrated governance of truth and
advancement. It has not yet demonstrated a complete prescriptive method for
choosing the work.
