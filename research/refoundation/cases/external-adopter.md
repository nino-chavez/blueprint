# External adopter evidence dossier

## Current evidentiary posture

The public registry identifies one de-named non-creator adopter. Blueprint's
validation and positioning artifacts describe this as categorically meaningful
0→1 adoption but explicitly singular and early. The underlying consumer corpus,
manifest, session history, and outcome receipts were not located in the current
workspace scan.

This dossier therefore establishes **adoption metadata only**. It cannot yet
support claims that the method transferred successfully, reduced operator
intervention, produced a product outcome, or was comprehensible without the
creator's tacit assistance.

## Candidate incident EA-01 — adoption is not effectiveness

**Status:** metadata-only · **Confidence:** high on distinction, unavailable on
outcome

- **Intent before:** demonstrate that Blueprint can be adopted outside the
  creator's own projects and tacit workspace.
- **Claim:** the first external adoption refutes "zero external users."
- **Evidence at the time:** an external operator adopted a bespoke,
  hand-bootstrapped rig. Public positioning accurately describes the evidence
  as one early adopter and warns against "reproducible/adopt-and-ship" claims.
- **Transition:** no method-effectiveness conclusion is admitted from the
  metadata alone.
- **Classification:** `positive-control` for adoption count; causal
  classification unavailable.
- **Earliest safe catch:** every external-adoption claim. Distinguish adoption,
  active use, independent operation, product outcome, and advocacy.
- **Candidate control to test:** adoption and outcome receipts have explicit
  definitions and observers; one cannot silently upgrade into another.
- **Counterevidence:** the engagement may contain rich evidence not present in
  the public repository. Absence from this scan is not evidence of absence.

## Evidence required for causal admission

At least:

1. initiating intent and pilot/actor definition;
2. how Blueprint was introduced and how much creator assistance was required;
3. one cold-resume or independent-operation observation;
4. a product/work outcome attributable at least partly to the method;
5. corrections or confusion encountered by the external operator;
6. the version/shape of Blueprint actually used; and
7. permission-safe, de-named citations that can live in this public corpus.

Until then, the external case remains a required gap in corpus ratification,
not a positive testimonial.
