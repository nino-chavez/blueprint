# Corpus ratification register

## Ratification — 2026-07-22

The operator instructed **continue** after the evidence-sprint handoff named the
six proposed defaults explicitly. This register records that continuation as
ratification of those defaults and authorization to derive the candidate
kernel. It does not authorize implementation, `template/` edits, consumer
migration, or a rename.

Accepted dispositions:

1. Film Room re-charters when distributable/no-engineer operation becomes an
   accepted initiative intent; a pay ask activates separate commercial claims.
2. Assisted beta and self-serve are distinct scope modes.
3. A solo operator may prove their own operational outcome, but cannot silently
   turn builder judgment into independent verification.
4. BC-04 remains conditional working-mode evidence, not a universal kernel
   requirement.
5. A checker that could not run cannot emit pass/fail evidence; the exact
   rendering of `could-not-observe` remains a kernel-design question.
6. The external adopter remains metadata-only until its underlying evidence is
   recovered.

## Purpose

Kernel derivation is deliberately blocked on this register. The dossiers
contain agent synthesis over source artifacts and sampled transcripts; the
operator must correct the causal record before those incidents become design
requirements.

Ratification means "accurate enough to use as falsification evidence." It does
not ratify any candidate control suggested by a dossier.

## Ratification outcome format

For each incident or question, record one of:

- `accept` — accurate enough for kernel falsification;
- `amend` — retain after the named correction;
- `demote` — useful supporting/conditional evidence, not kernel input;
- `reject` — causal account is wrong or insufficiently evidenced; or
- `needs-source` — no judgment until the named evidence is recovered.

No kernel proposal should be authored while the minimum incident set remains
entirely `operator review`.
