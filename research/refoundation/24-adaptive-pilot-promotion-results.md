# Adaptive Commerce Content pilot-promotion results

## Outcome

The prospective external pilot closes the live-evidence gap for the version-1
execution boundary and exposes two narrower controls worth retaining in root
research.

It does **not** authorize a public Blueprint steering command or a stamped
methodology change. The new delegation and holdout findings each have one
prospective consumer instance; the canonical amendment convention treats two
similar initiative findings as the strong-promotion threshold.

## What the live pilot established

At exact consumer history:

1. The evaluator selected one ready external boundary: authenticated
   BigCommerce sandbox context.
2. The operator supplied that context in signed-in Chrome.
3. The agent resumed, completed the authorized read-only email-template
   observation, closed the editor without saving, and returned to machine work.
4. After local evidence was complete, Blueprint selected a genuine product
   disposition boundary.
5. The operator did not choose among the four local options; the operator
   delegated the reversible selection while retaining external and expansion
   authority.
6. Blueprint recorded its recommended local direction without treating that act
   as product acceptance, merchant evidence, deployment authority, or sandbox
   mutation.
7. A late POC remained sealed until the independent direction and comparison
   rubric were committed. Its later inspection retained the direction and
   added named hardening requirements.

The completed version-1 packet evaluates `VALID` and selects `hold`: 15 active
claims, one historical claim, zero unresolved incident clusters, two of two
operator touches, and no handoff.

## Question A result — bounded delegated decisions

`blueprint-steering/2` makes the pilot's local decision delegation executable
without changing K1.

A decision claim now declares a class and effects. An autonomous decision route
must reference an authored delegation whose:

- exact satisfied human authorization claim and revision match;
- delegate matches the route owner;
- decision class covers the claim;
- allowed effects cover every effect;
- prohibited effects cover none;
- method-default policy permits selection;
- decision record matches the route capture; and
- completed decision has a matching disposition and exercise receipt.

The evaluator rejects missing delegation, undeclared class, prohibited external
effect, human-claim automation, mismatched decision record, and missing exercise
receipt. A version-2 decision without delegation remains an operator route.
Version 0 and version 1 outputs remain valid.

The steering suite passes **129 assertions**. The sanitized delegation fixture
evaluates:

```text
delegated-local-decision: VALID; next=record-disposition; active=3; historical=1; clusters=0; touches=1/1; handoff=no; owners=blueprint-agent
```

### Honest limit

The steering packet does not contain the K1 actor registry. It can prove that a
route is covered by an authored delegation and satisfied human authorization
claim; it cannot independently prove that the named delegating actor held
kernel authority. A future adapter must resolve that reference against the
governing charter rather than inventing authority in the recipe layer.

## Question B result — source influence and holdouts

`blueprint-source-influence/0` defines four roles with fixed phase/use policy:

- `definition-input`;
- `prior-art`;
- `holdout`; and
- `receipt-only`.

A holdout requires its pinned source revision, independent baseline revision,
pre-unseal protocol, exact unseal decision/revision, sealed state transition,
and contamination disclosure. Typed artifact citations cannot:

- use the holdout before unsealing;
- cite it from a pre-unseal decision;
- relabel it as definition input after unsealing; or
- use receipt-only evidence to define the initiative.

The source-influence suite passes **26 assertions**. Its sanitized Adaptive
Commerce Content fixture evaluates:

```text
adaptive-content-holdout: VALID; sources=4; artifacts=7; citations=7; holdouts=1
```

### Honest limit

The linter proves consistency of authored declarations. It cannot prove that a
person or agent never saw the held-out content through another channel, nor can
it derive git ancestry from arbitrary revision identifiers. Repository history,
sealed-access discipline, and explicit contamination disclosure remain
evidence.

## Method assessment after the pilot

The pilot supports a narrower and more accurate conclusion than “Blueprint ran
on its own”:

- the evaluator successfully prevented premature operator interruption and
  resumed after a real external boundary;
- operator ownership did not need to mean operator selection of every
  reversible local branch;
- explicit delegation reduced steering without broadening authority;
- a source's provenance was insufficient to control when it could influence the
  initiative; and
- a sealed holdout made independent convergence and divergence distinguishable.

It also exposed remaining product obligations:

- authoring version 2 is too verbose for public use;
- delegation must bind to K1 actor authority through an adapter;
- source influence needs repository-aware authoring/lint integration;
- neither contract has upgrade, doctor, fleet, or support behavior; and
- a second contrasting consumer has not yet reproduced either pattern.

## Disposition

Accept the two controls as root experimental candidates. Keep the completed
Adaptive Commerce Content consumer frozen. Do not retroactively rewrite its
version-1 packet or mark its amendments “already promoted.”

The next qualifying work is a second prospective consumer that needs one of the
same controls. Preregister the relevant contract before source access or
delegated choice, then test cold authoring and interpretation. Only after that
evidence should Blueprint decide whether to:

1. absorb delegation into the compact/K1 adapter and expose an opt-in steering
   command;
2. ship source influence as a research-module linter; or
3. keep either pattern as initiative-local policy.

There is no methodology wave or consumer-sync action from this result because
no distribution surface changed.
