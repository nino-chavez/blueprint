# Feedback — "D.", casual-visitor persona, 2026-06-09 (Slack DM)

First organic stakeholder feedback on the live portal (blueprint-platform.pages.dev).
Unprompted; a colleague (non-practitioner) found the link and tried to
understand the product cold. Identity anonymized for the public repo —
verbatim original retained in `feedback/raw/` (gitignored, local only).

## The exchange (condensed)

- D. opened the conversation with the link and a declared intent: "I want to
  understand this."
- She opened the site and bounced inside one visit — it demanded more
  attention than a casual visit had to give ("not going to allow me to dig
  in").
- The author offered the reflex remediation — ask an agent ("Claude, how do I
  use this?") — and she explicitly declined it: "I don't want to replace real
  humans with claude."

## What this is (in methodology terms)

A **bounce report** — the most valuable feedback class per the Mom Test: a real
person reporting a real problem, unprompted, with zero compliment padding. Two
distinct signals:

1. **The front door failed a seconds-budget comprehension JTBD.** She arrived
   *wanting* to understand ("I want to understand this") and bounced inside one
   visit. The portal currently serves an evaluator willing to dig; she is a
   curious non-practitioner with a single-scroll attention budget — a persona
   the self-app's research never modeled.
2. **The assumed remediation path was rejected.** The author's reflex answer
   ("ask Claude") is the product's implicit fallback for confused visitors —
   and she explicitly declined it. "Confused visitors will self-serve via an
   agent" is a disconfirmed hypothesis, not a safety net.
