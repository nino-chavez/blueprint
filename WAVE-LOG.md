bound to `--tier` (T3 strict+two-role+coverage-gate / T2 co-deliverable+executed / T1 non-trivial-logic-only). **(4) the detective backstop:** a per-AC × per-test-type coverage matrix (functional/integration/E2E/BDD → not-defined / defined-but-failing / green), derivable only once tests carry the `acs:`/`@ac:` tag (so tagging is the prerequisite; untagged-test is itself a finding).
